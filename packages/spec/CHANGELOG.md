# Changelog

All notable changes to `@xochi/spec` are documented here. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), versions follow [SemVer](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **ERC-8262 provider protocol** (SPEC.md §10, xochi-sdk#37): `erc8262/provider-discovery.schema.json` for `/.well-known/erc8262-provider.json`, `erc8262/provider-evidence.schema.json` for `/v1/erc8262/evidence/{evidenceId}`, and `erc8262/vectors.json` (+ `vectors.schema.json`), generated by `npm run generate:erc8262`; `test/erc8262-provider.test.ts` validates the set with `ajv` (new devDependency) and re-derives every outcome. Signing is out of scope.
  - Vectors: native, gateway, alias and impostor fixtures (26 discovery, 49 evidence), 25 schema rejections, 205 evaluation cases and 18 quorum cases, 31 refusal codes.
  - Identifiers: `sourceId`, `claimType`, `trustDomainId` (never an IP literal) and `attestorProviderId` (the Oracle `providerId`, at most 2^256 - 1). Protocol constants ship in `vectors.json` `limits`.
  - Neither document is signed: the consumer's allowlist pins each trust domain to its attestor per deployment, evidence must come from the discovery host, and acceptance authorizes nothing until the Oracle-verified proof it feeds passes `proof_mismatch` and `proof_stale` (SPEC.md §10.5 steps 30-31). Every evaluation reads the Oracle afresh at the latest finalized block at or after the proof's inclusion; the reads that can only refuse (steps 21, 23 and step 31's revocation) are also made at the latest block, and a refusal at either block refuses. Step 30 checks provenance (the `publicInputs` words hash to the Oracle record's `publicInputsHash`, whose `subject` and `proofType` match), identity (attestor and artifact for type 4, the `keyId` key's signer hash for 7/8/9, the submitter for all) and the consumer's statement, which names the proof type it is for (none for type 4; jurisdictions and `config_hash` for 7/9, least `threshold_m` for 9; for 8 the risk proof type, `bound_lower`, `config_hash` and the one word the circuit constrains in that mode: `direction` for a threshold, `bound_upper` for a range). Step 23 also re-reads `isValidConfig` for the `config_hash` of the statement for the evidence's proof type. Quorum counts distinct attestors per (`claimType`, `proofType`), each member's proof proving the requirement's statement, against one snapshot of consumer state plus the revocations any member records, keyed as they are recorded.
  - An announced `evidenceId` is checked against its schema rule before the URL is built, is never logged in full or sent as a referrer, and the body must carry it. Fetch contract: https, no redirects, 10 s timeout, 64 KiB / 8 KiB body caps, no cached copies, `Cache-Control: no-store` on evidence. A `410` (served only for issued ids, and never by a hosting platform for a removed deployment) and a schema-valid revoked body carrying the announced id are final and recorded under (fetch host, announced id), never under body fields, whatever the discovery fetch returned; `404`, every other failure and a schema-failing `200` are unavailable.
  - Rotation and time: one 60 s clock-skew rule (SPEC.md §10.5, Clocks). Providers announce `notAfter` ahead and switch the Oracle at it; a signed-signals successor may overlap its predecessor (hash registered first), which still ends at `notAfter`; type 4 evidence lives only while its root is valid on the Oracle (48 h); evidence lives at most 365 days. Denied attestors are refused (`attestor_denied`).
  - Observation store (SPEC.md §10.7): durable, shared, atomic and monotonic, with read-your-writes; the first document recorded for an id stays (a misdeployed id is re-issued under a new id); key revocations are remembered by `keyId` and material per trust domain and, recorded under the attestor pinned when they were recorded, by material for every trust domain pinned to that attestor now, never across attestors. Revocations of ids the consumer granted on are never bounded or dropped; the others MAY be bounded per (trust domain, announcing party as the consumer authenticated it, never the subject wallet) at no fewer than `limits.minUngrantedRevocationsPerAnnouncer` (10 000), and overflow makes only that party's new announcements unavailable. Every evaluation case pins what it writes (`expected.records`, SPEC.md §10.8) and what it does to a held grant (`expected.heldGrant`), and `follows` chains cases. Held grants are re-evaluated every `revocationCheckSeconds` against the granted proof; a refusal at step 1-5, 18, 29 or 31 ends the grant, any other refusal or a re-evaluation that cannot complete suspends it until it ends at the earlier of the evidence's and the attestation's expiry.
- **Moved in from the archived `axol-io/riddler-sdk`** (snapshot, no history) as `@xochi/spec` (was `@riddler/spec`). Every vector digest, signature, typehash and domain separator is byte-identical; only `description` strings naming a package changed. EIP-712 domain and contract names (`Xochi`, `RiddlerSolver`, `RiddlerFiller`, `RiddlerEscrow`, `SolverBond`) are unchanged.
- `README.md`: how to consume the spec (moved from the repo root README).
- **`conformance/shielded.json` + `shielded.schema.json`** (xochi#633, xochi#635): vectors for the shielded settlement contract v1 typed encodings approved 2026-10-18 (xochi `shielded-execution.md` §7), with the `NetworkIdentity` encoding `NetworkIdentity(uint256 aztecChainId,uint256 protocolVersion,address rollupAddress,uint256 l1ChainId,bytes32 genesisOrNetworkDigest)` approved by the product owner on 2026-10-19 (`protocolVersion` = the numeric Aztec rollup version; the §7 record lands in a separate xochi PR). Covers the `NetworkIdentity` hashStruct, the salted `Domain (binding)` (`Xochi Shielded Recipient`) and `Domain (terms)` (`Xochi Shielded Terms`), `RecipientBinding` struct hashes, digests and source-wallet signatures (public test keys), and refusal cases for Aztec `bytes32` values (zero, not below BN254 r; magnitude alone, since EVM/Aztec provenance is enforced by descriptor/address derivation) and amounts (`0 < x <= 2^128 - 1`, canonical decimal). The generator imports nothing from `@xochi/signer`: it derives every value from §7's literals with ethers primitives and writes its refusal expectations as data, and `@xochi/signer` is tested against the committed file. Regenerated by `npm run generate:shielded`, gated in CI. No vector for ShieldedTermsV1, manifestDigest, descriptorDigest or the recipient message: §7 leaves them open, and the file's `open` array names them. The recipient message is open in its own right: selecting the recipient account class (xochi#671) is a prerequisite, not its specification. As of 2026-10-21 the class is pinned for Aztec SDK 5.2.0 networks, and the pin for the tracer's network (public testnet, 6.0.0-rc) is open; the `open` reason cites §7 instead of carrying that status, so it does not go stale when the pin moves. Network, corridor and Aztec values are fixtures. Additive; no existing vector changes.

### Changed

- **Version 0.9.0** (MINOR, additive; SPEC.md §0.3). Nothing between it and `0.8.0` was published, so it also carries the unreleased `0.8.1` (PATCH, no wire or vector change; it moved the version past every pre-fix commit, which declare the never-published `0.8.0`). No taker signed struct, fee artifact, served wire field or existing vector changes. Riddler must advertise `spec_version: "0.9.0"` in lockstep, or the nightly `spec_version` probe reports DRIFT. The `@xochi/sdk` and `@xochi/reference` dev pins move to `0.9.0`.
- **Wire contract refreshed to served spec 0.8.0** (from axol-io/riddler-sdk#150): `wire/openapi.{json,sha256}` replaced by the prod refresh (Canton `ChainAddress` branch, chain `253340876`, `cvm`/`canton_party`, escrow schemas, `escrow_refused`/`escrow_unavailable`); SPEC.md §3.2/§3.3/§3.5/§6 follow the served shapes.
- **Wire contract refreshed again (2026-10-12, `0 BREAKING, 2 STALE`)**: the served `0.8.0` gained xochi#584's optional `QuoteRequest.promo_discount_bps` and `LayeredFeeBreakdown.discount { source, bps, amount }`; `wire/openapi.{json,sha256}` carry them and SPEC.md §3.3/§4.5 describe them. No vector, schedule or reconciliation rule changes, and no version of its own: it ships in `0.9.0` (SPEC.md §0.3).
- **SOLVER-RFC `0.5.0-draft`** (clarification, `rfcVersion` unchanged, from axol-io/riddler-sdk#151): the quote-time `minOutput` is the quote less a venue-wide announce margin (`SOLVER_ANNOUNCE_FLOOR_BPS`), never a taker-chosen value such as `slippage_bps`.
- **SOLVER-RFC `0.5.0-draft`: the announcement is public-only** (producer withdrawal, `rfcVersion` unchanged, xochi-sdk#29 with Riddler #1200). The SDK-side narrowing ships here. The producer withdrawal is `[PROPOSED]` until Riddler #1200 (PR #1302) merges to `master` (the RFC legend's meaning of `[IMPLEMENTED]`). Separately, the privacy property holds only once it is deployed and the feed has drained. §§2.2/4.2 narrow `IntentAnnouncement.settlementPreference` to the single required value `"public"` and withdraw `recipientCommitment`; §2.4 admits private flow back only under a separately reviewed encrypted or commit-reveal design. Rollout order: (0) the venue must serve `rfcVersion` `0.5.0-draft` (Riddler PR #1245, open; Riddler still serves `0.4.0-draft`, which every `0.5.0-draft` SDK refuses); (1) the producer withdraws; (2) old publishers and feed rings drain; (3) the narrowed `@xochi/solver-sdk` is published. A solver running it earlier on `HttpSolverTransport` MUST set `onInvalidEvent`; without it, a retained non-public entry ends `SolverClient.run`. A solver with an injected `SolverTransport` cannot set `onInvalidEvent` and must instead validate each event with `parseSolverEvent` in its transport. Consumer filtering is defence in depth, not the privacy boundary. Taker settlement types (`SPEC.md`) and every vector are unchanged, and it takes no version of its own (it ships in `0.9.0`). The `@xochi/solver-sdk` dev pin moves to `0.5.0`.
- `package.json` gains description, keywords and homepage.
- `package.json` declares `repository` with its `directory`, as `npm publish --provenance` requires; releases publish from `.github/workflows/release.yml` with provenance on a `spec-v*` tag.
- `engines.node` is `>=22`. `prepublishOnly` runs `format:check`, `typecheck` and `test` before every publish. The tarball ships this `CHANGELOG.md`.
- SPEC.md no longer links `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, which exists nowhere and shipped in no tarball; §5.2 and §9 still cite it by name.

### Fixed

- **Wire differ** (`wire/compare.mjs`): local `$ref` parameters and request bodies are resolved before grading served-only ones, so a required one is BREAKING, and every served `$ref` that is external, dangling, cyclic or malformed grades BREAKING wherever it sits (it graded STALE outside served-only parameters and request bodies); fragments decode in RFC 6901 §6 order.
- **`generate:wire`** grades against the `openapi.{json,sha256}` pair committed at HEAD, never the working tree, so a gutted, re-digested local copy cannot launder a BREAKING change; a committed pair that fails its digest is refused with no flag; `--first-vendoring` consents only to an unreadable or unrecognisable baseline (`unrecognisedContract`, shared with `verify:openapi`) and never skips the diff against an intact one; the document is renamed before the digest.
- Generator main-guards compare real file URLs, so `npm run generate:*` no longer no-ops in paths needing URL encoding.
